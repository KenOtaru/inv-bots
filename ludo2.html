NAIJA LUDO RULES

  • Start: Roll a 6 to move a piece out of the yard.
  • 6 Bonus: Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
  • Triple 6: Three 6s in a row ends your turn immediately!
  • Capture: Land on an opponent to send them home. Capture gives a bonus roll!
  • Safety: Stars and colored start squares are safe zones.
  • Win: Move all 4 pieces to the center triangle.
