Naija Ludo Rules

  • 2 Dice: Roll to move.
  • Start: You need a 6 to move a piece out of the yard.
  • Movement: Click a piece to move it. You can use dice values individually.
  • Capture: Land on an opponent to send them home (and get a bonus roll).
  • Win: Get all 4 pieces to the center triangle.
